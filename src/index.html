Saturday & Sunday
i
Weekend return trips start from Nassau Street and Hilton Garden (IFSC), not UCD — the only two return times Sillan publishes. The bus does call at every other stop on the way back to Cootehill, but individual times for them aren't published, so they're shown as TBC.
i
On Sundays, the Dublin-bound service continues past Dunshaughlin into the city, dropping off at O'Connell Street, Nassau Street, St Stephen's Green, and UCD (no times published for these). Saturday's onward routing past Dunshaughlin isn't detailed on Sillan's timetable.
+
Bank Holidays: no Sunday service on a Bank Holiday Sunday. On a Bank Holiday Monday, the Sunday timetable runs instead.

What's the same every day

6 of 8 departures towards Dublin and 5 of 8 departures from Dublin run identically Monday through Friday — the core daytime service doesn't change.

Where Friday differs

Mon–Thu adds an early 06:25 run and a 09:15 Navan-starting run. Friday evening is lighter: last UCD departure is 17:30, with a single 19:00 Nassau Street-only run replacing Mon–Thu's later 18:10 and 19:15 services.

About this page

This is an unofficial companion page for Sillan Coaches' Route 179 timetable (Cootehill / Kingscourt ⇄ Dublin / UCD). It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Sillan Coaches — always check sillan.ie or call +353 42 9669130 to confirm times before travelling.

Times are transcribed from Sillan's published posters and website. Sections marked TBC (DCU) aren't confirmed yet. Each column header states which days it runs (e.g. "Mon–Thu").

More information will be added here over time.

All stop extra info is best-approx. location or nearest TFI bus stop. You may need to be on the opposite side of the road from it.
